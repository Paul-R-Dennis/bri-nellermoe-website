Published — Embers in Time · Book One

Secrets in Embers

Gaisha was once a vast and diverse realm, beloved and cherished by all who shared her. Until war ravaged the once thriving home…and she fell. Split into seven, her history was nearly erased save for the few who remember. The few who hold on.

Centuries later, deep in the ancient forests of Calatheas, the two unlikeliest of souls—she of the Witches and he of the Fae—found one another. Despite his family hunting hers for power, they fell in love and had a dream to reunite their home, once and for all. That is, until a long-ago promise was broken, and the son of Fae was forced to watch as a curse befell the daughter of the Earth.

A thousand years later, Eve Calloway has spent her entire life living with the secret of being a witch. What’s worse is that she’s been plagued by nightmares for as long as she can remember. As she’s grown, they’ve become more vivid, more lucid, more tangible than ever before. When new neighbors move in at the same time attacks begin to occur, threatening the safety of their home, it leaves Eve questioning if they truly are just nightmares. Or if the inferno she sees is a warning of what’s to come.
