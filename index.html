Embers in Time · Book One

Secrets in Embers

Eve Calloway has spent her entire life living with the secret of being a witch. What’s worse is that she’s been plagued by nightmares for as long as she can remember. As she’s grown, they’ve become more vivid, more lucid, more tangible than ever before. When new neighbors move in at the same time attacks begin to occur, threatening the safety of their home, it leaves Eve questioning if they truly are just nightmares. Or if the inferno she sees is a warning of what’s to come.
